The Long Now

Everything else on this dashboard is about right now, right here. This is the same spot, at every scale of time and space.
Everything in "recorded history" — every book, every war, every name anyone has ever written down — happens in the last hair's-breadth of this line. The line itself is 13.8 billion years long. Positions above are on a logarithmic scale (equal distances represent equal multiples of time, not equal amounts) — a true-to-scale linear version would put all of human history into a space thinner than this text.
